Liner notes

It started with one speaker on a hill.

In 2018, J. Kirven carried a Bluetooth speaker up Libby Hill Park to play music and watch the sun go down. People kept showing up. In 2022 the ritual became Electric Sun Society — a nonprofit creative collective for African electronic music, visual art and wellness in Richmond.
