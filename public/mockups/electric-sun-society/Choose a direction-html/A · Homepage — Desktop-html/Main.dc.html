Our story
2018.

One Bluetooth speaker. One hill. One sunset — and Richmond kept showing up.

Vibes At Sunset began when J. Kirven brought a speaker to Libby Hill Park to play music and watch the sun go down. In 2022 the ritual became Electric Sun Society — a nonprofit creative collective for African electronic music, visual art and wellness.

~200at the early gatherings
2,000+on the hill, August 2026
$0admission, every Vibes this season
Oct 8 · The finale moves

New hill.
Same sun.

Vibes outgrew Libby Hill. Years after one speaker on the grass, more than 2,000 of you were on the slope — so the season finale moves to the Virginia War Memorial: a plaza above the James with room for everybody and parking of its own.
