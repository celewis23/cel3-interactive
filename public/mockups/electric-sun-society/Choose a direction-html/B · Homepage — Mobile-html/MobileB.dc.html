A letter from the collective

Dear Richmond —
In 2018 one speaker went up Libby Hill so a few friends could watch the sun go down. You kept coming. By August there were more than 2,000 of you on that slope. The hill got too small for all of us, so the finale moves to the Virginia War Memorial.
New hill. Same sun. Still free.

— Electric Sun Society
