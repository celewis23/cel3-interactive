A letter from the collective

Dear Richmond —
In 2018 one speaker went up Libby Hill so a few friends could watch the sun go down. You kept coming. By August there were more than 2,000 of you on that slope. The hill got too small for all of us, so for the finale we’re moving to the Virginia War Memorial.
New hill. Same sun. Still free. See you on the 8th.

— Electric Sun Society

What’s on the hill

Four hours, 5 to 9. Bring a blanket. Bring your people.

Sound

Amapiano, Afro house and Afro-tech from J. Kirven and guest selectors.

Body

Yoga and wellness on the lawn before the dance floor takes over.

Art

Live visual art and local artists — a vibe you can actually see.

Table

Food trucks and local vendors — Richmond’s best, all in one field.
