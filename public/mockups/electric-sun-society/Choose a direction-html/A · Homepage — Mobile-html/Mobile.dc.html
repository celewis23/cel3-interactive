Our story
2018.

One Bluetooth speaker. One hill. One sunset — and Richmond kept showing up.

J. Kirven brought a speaker to Libby Hill Park to watch the sun go down. In 2022 the ritual became Electric Sun Society — a nonprofit collective for African electronic music, art and wellness.
