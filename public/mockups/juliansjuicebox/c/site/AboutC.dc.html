From personal renewal to a juice business.

Juice Box — From Personal Renewal to a Thriving Juice Business with Julian Ramirez · a short film by Nama

Julian's Juicebox didn't start as a business plan. It started with Julian Ramirez going after better health for himself.

That search led him to juicing, and juicing turned into a passion. The more it did for him, the more he wanted other people to feel it too — so the passion became Julian's Juicebox.

Today the mission is the same one he began with: helping other people find their own renewal through fresh, cold-pressed juice.
