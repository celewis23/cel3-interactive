FAQs

Where do you ship?

We ship to the 48 contiguous states. Orders take 1–3 days to process, then 1–3 days in transit. Shipments go out Monday through Wednesday.

How do the juices travel?

Juices ship frozen in 2-inch styrofoam-lined boxes.

How long do they keep?

Keep frozen for up to 6 months.

How do I thaw them?

Move to the fridge 24–48 hours before drinking, or use the counter or a warm water bowl for a faster thaw.

How often should I cleanse?

Each comes with a Juice Cleanse Guide. We suggest a 3-day cleanse every other month or a 1–2 day cleanse monthly, and fresh juice daily.
