FAQs

Where do you ship, and how long does it take?

We ship to the 48 contiguous states. Orders take 1–3 days to process, then 1–3 days in transit. Shipments go out Monday through Wednesday.

How do the juices travel?

Juices ship frozen in 2-inch styrofoam-lined boxes.

How long can I store them?

Keep them frozen for up to 6 months.

How do I thaw my juice?

Move it to the fridge 24–48 hours before drinking, or use the counter or a warm water bowl for a faster thaw.

How often should I cleanse?

Every cleanse comes with a Juice Cleanse Guide. We suggest a 3-day cleanse every other month or a 1–2 day cleanse monthly, and fresh juice daily.
