Our story

It started with one Yorkie, and a daughter leaving home.

When our daughter moved into her own place, we wanted to send a piece of home with her — so she got a Yorkie puppy of her own. We named her Honey. What we didn't expect was how much peace that one small dog would bring into a big life change, for her and for us.

Word got around. Friends wanted their own Honey. Then friends of friends. What began as a gift became Frith Yorkies — a small, family-run operation raising Yorkies, Toy Poodles, Corkies, Yorkipoos and Spaniels with the same care we gave our first girl. Every litter is raised underfoot in our home, not in a kennel, so your puppy already knows what it's like to be loved.
