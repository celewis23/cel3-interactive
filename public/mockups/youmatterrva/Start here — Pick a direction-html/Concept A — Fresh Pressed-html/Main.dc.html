How the reset works

  1. BeforeTwo to three days out, cut back on caffeine, processed foods, added sugar and alcohol. Notice how your body and head space feel. A small journal comes in your kit.
  2. DuringOne juice every two hours, six a day, each followed by at least 16oz of water. Hungry? Reach for seeds, nuts, watermelon or raw veggies. Read your daily affirmations, meditate, move lightly.
  3. AfterYou did it. Ease back in with soups, leafy salads, healthy fats, seeds, nuts, fresh fruit and vegetables.
Our story

The name is the whole point.

Tricia Walker started with a mobile juice cart, then brought it home to MacArthur Avenue in 2024. Everything on the menu is made to take care of your body — and the name is there to take care of the rest.

Every visit comes with a reminder that you matter, and the counter keeps mental-health resources out in the open for anyone who needs them.
