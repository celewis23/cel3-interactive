## Directions

- **A · Concert Hall**: navy, gold, ivory; serif; elegant
- **B · Black & White Keys**: black, white and red; grotesk; square; video hero
- **C · Bright & Playful**: yellow and blue; rounded; made for kids and families

Each direction has a website, 6 family-app screens and 8 studio-dashboard screens.

## Media notes

- `site-b-hero-loop-STOCK-PREVIEW-REPLACE.mp4` is an **Adobe Stock watermarked preview**, for the proposal only. Replace it with the studio's own footage, or a licensed clip, before going public.
- The spotlight videos were compressed for the web (720p). Keep the original phone files if you want higher quality later.
- The videos include students who are minors. Keep the proposal unlisted / `noindex`, and confirm parents are OK with their child appearing on a public site.
