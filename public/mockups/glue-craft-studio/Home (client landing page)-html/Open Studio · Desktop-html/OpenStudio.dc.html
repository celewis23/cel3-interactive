An arts & crafts space for all

Materials with a second life.

A safe space for people to spark their creativity and share the experience. With a strong focus on reuse, Glue Craft Studio sources materials that would have been discarded or gone unused and works them into the crafting. It expands the life of the object, keeps prices accessible and reduces waste.

Hello from Amy & Vanessa

Lifelong crafters, hoping to honor those who taught us.

Amy
Vanessa
Amy

Amy remembers crafting with her Grandma, Tutu, at the family dinner table, tinkering away for hours together. She now leads our Kids Club, with a bachelors in Youth Development & Camp Management and years of experience working with kids.

Vanessa

Vanessa attributes her love of crafting to her mom, Nora, who always had a new craft ready for every childhood hangout or sleepover.

"There really is nothing more special and healing than taking the time to learn a new skill and explore your creative side, especially in a shared experience."
