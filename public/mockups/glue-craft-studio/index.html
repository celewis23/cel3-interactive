Started at the kitchen table

Two lifelong crafters, honoring the people who taught them

Amy learned at her grandmother's table, tinkering for hours after dinner. Vanessa's love of making came from her mom, who always had a new project ready for a rainy afternoon.

Glue Craft Studio is our way of passing that down — a space where people slow down, work with their hands, and make something real together, using materials that would have otherwise gone to waste.

What a class actually looks like

Every kids' session moves through the same four stages, built around how kids actually focus and learn.

1

Think & brainstorm

Talk through the project and start problem-solving before any glue comes out.

2

Craft

Hands-on building — fine motor skills and following a project through to the end.

3

Play

Test it out, move around, and enjoy the thing you just made with the group.

4

Wind down

A quiet close to talk about what worked, what didn't, and what's next.

Questions people ask

The short version — reach out if yours isn't here.

Do I need to bring my own supplies? +

No — everything is included in the cost of registration. Just bring a willingness to get a little messy.

How big are the classes? +

We cap every class at 10 kids so everyone gets real attention from an instructor.

What if a class gets canceled? +

You'll hear from us as soon as possible and your class will be rescheduled — no questions asked.

Is the studio open yet? +

Our Church Hill studio space opens summer 2026. Classes, kits, and events are running in the meantime.

Can adults join too? +

Yes — open studio sessions, workshops, and evening events are all built for grown-ups who want to make something too.
