Room policies.

We want your experience to be seamless from start to finish. Here's what to keep in mind when booking a private suite.

Min. 8 guestsA minimum of 8 guests is required to reserve any private suite.
One check per roomEach suite is billed under a single room check for a smooth experience.
Pay as you orderIndividual checks must be paid as orders are placed — no splitting a pile of checks at the end. We appreciate your understanding!
All-you-can-eatAll-you-can-eat options are available for private party bookings. Ask your server for details.
Decorate awayYou're welcome to decorate your suite. All decorations must be taken down by the end of your party.
No refunds or discountsWe don't offer refunds or discounts on large party bookings. Large parties are our specialty — we aim to ensure every guest leaves pleased!
20% gratuityA 20% gratuity may be added to each party for private suite reservations.
