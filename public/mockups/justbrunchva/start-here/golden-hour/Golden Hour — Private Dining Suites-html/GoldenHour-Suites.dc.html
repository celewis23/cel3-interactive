Good to know

Room policies

We want your experience to be seamless from start to finish. Here's what to keep in mind when booking a private suite.

Minimum 8 guestsA minimum of 8 guests is required to reserve any private suite.
One check per roomEach suite is billed under a single room check for a smooth experience.
Pay as you orderIndividual checks must be paid as orders are placed. This helps us avoid splitting multiple checks at the end of your party — we appreciate your understanding!
All-you-can-eat availableAll-you-can-eat options are available for private party bookings. Ask your server for details.
Decorating welcomeYou are welcome to decorate your suite for your event. All decorations must be taken down by the end of your party.
No refunds or discountsWe do not offer refunds or discounts on large party bookings. Large parties are our specialty and we do them well — we aim to ensure every guest leaves pleased!
20% gratuityA 20% gratuity may be added to each party for private suite reservations.
