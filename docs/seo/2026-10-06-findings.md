# CEL3 Interactive SEO audit — 6 October 2026

Status: initial crawl findings, recorded before SEO code changes. Public canonical host currently resolves to `https://cel3interactive.com`. The email/attachment and admin Messages changes are a separate, completed release (`0ac1635`).

## Crawl scope and evidence

Fetched the live sitemap and robots endpoints on both hosts, followed HTTP/HTTPS redirect variants, crawled the public navigation graph, and compared it with the app route inventory. The initial crawl fetched 29 URLs representing 28 unique pages: 22 marketing pages and six utility/private-entry pages. All 22 marketing pages returned 200 with a heading and substantive body copy in server HTML. There are no missing marketing titles or descriptions, and no accidental marketing `noindex` directives in this sample.

Client preview HTML under `/mockups/`, protected admin/portal routes, and token-based contracts/estimates/forms are not marketing landing pages. Their indexing policies are reviewed separately; private records and access tokens will not be published in this report.

The business is described as “Richmond-based” in the current homepage source and live copy. Street address, public phone, founding year, service area and Google Business Profile eligibility require confirmation. A city-only claim is not evidence for a street address, legal business name, hours, or a founding date.

## Review boundaries

- Technical metadata, indexing, structured data and performance fixes can proceed in the SEO PR.
- New city/service pages and substantial sales copy remain proposals/drafts.
- Google Business Profile descriptions, categories and posts remain drafts.
- AI-specific allow/block rules are pending the owner's explicit choice. Search crawling and model-training crawling are different controls; Google's `Google-Extended` also covers certain grounding uses outside Google Search.
- Lab performance measurements must be labelled as lab results. They do not establish a 28-day field Core Web Vitals pass or a real-user INP result.
